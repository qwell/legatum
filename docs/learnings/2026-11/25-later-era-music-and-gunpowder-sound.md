## 2026-11-25: Later-era music, the sea and gunpowder-age battle sounds: the sources a cloud session can reach

- **What happened.** The first sound pass (0 A.D.) left guns, cannon, aircraft and the sea silent, and every era played ancient-world music. The usual free-audio sites (Freesound, OpenGameArt, incompetech, Wikimedia) are blocked from a cloud session.
- **What is reachable.** `archive.ubuntu.com` and exact paths on `raw.githubusercontent.com`. A packaged open-source game is the best source: its `debian/copyright` states the licence per path, which is a verifiable record. FreeCol's music is CC BY 4.0 and Unknown Horizons' content is CC BY-SA 3.0; both are age-of-sail scores. Unciv mirrors Freesound recordings with per-file licences in `docs/Credits.md`, and ESC-50 lists a licence per clip (use only the CC0 ones: the dataset as a whole is non-commercial).
- **Music by era** (`feel/sound.ts::musicPool`). A manifest entry may carry `eras: [first, last]` (1-based). The 0 A.D. music is eras 1 to 3, the new music era 4 on. A people's opening track ignores `eras`, so a people keeps its own voice all game. A new era changes the pool, never the track that is playing (`setMusicForView` only stores the era).
- **Gotcha: track titles are player text.** The Guide's Credits list them, and "Royal Troops" failed the glossary test ("Troops" is another name for military unit). The track was swapped, not the test.
- **Gotcha: run order.** `build_audio.py` rewrites the manifest and `import_0ad.py` keeps only non-0 A.D. entries plus its own, so `build_later.py` runs last. It also stamps `eras` on the 0 A.D. music.
- **Not done.** Nobody has listened to it. The gunshot and cannon are 24 kHz MP3 copies, the sea is a 9-second loop, the later eras have no music of their own century, and the Aztecs' two tracks are New World pieces from a colonisation game, not Mesoamerican music.
