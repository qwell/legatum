# Music by people, land sound and battle sound (2026-11-25)

- **The cloud session can reach few audio sources** (corrected the same day: `archive.ubuntu.com` is reachable too, and its packaged games gave the later-era music; see `25-later-era-music-and-gunpowder-sound.md`). freesound, archive.org, kenney.nl, opengameart and Wikimedia are blocked by the proxy; `raw.githubusercontent.com/0ad/0ad/master/...` works with exact paths. So this pass is all 0 A.D. (CC BY-SA 3.0, credit Wildfire Games with links to wildfiregames.com and the licence). 0 A.D. stops before gunpowder and has no sea ambience: guns, cannon, shells, aircraft and the sea are silent on purpose, and later eras and the Aztecs have no music of their own yet. A second source has to be fetched from a machine with open network.
- **Music is a playlist now, not two loops.** The pool comes from the manifest (`mood`, `peoples`), a people's first listed track opens its game, a track that ends waits 4 s and hands over to another, and war swaps pools with the old 3 s crossfade. Hot seat passes no people, or the music would change at every handoff.
- **Battle sound reads the same `CombatPlan` the map draws** (`feel/battleSound.ts`), scheduled on the audio clock from the animation's start time, so sound and picture cannot drift. It is capped at 10 cues a turn and merges the same sound inside 160 ms. It follows the map-animation setting: no animation, no battle sound.
- **Short one-shots have unreliable loudness readings**, and the 0 A.D. takes differ by 10 dB. Each battle file carries a `gain` in the manifest worked out from its measured loudness (target -24 LUFS, a little under the interface sounds).
- **Land loops are decoded to mono on purpose**: a 40 s stereo buffer is 14 MB of memory; mono halves it, and only the land the camera has visited is decoded.
- `build_audio.py` rewrites `manifest.json`; run `import_0ad.py` after it.
