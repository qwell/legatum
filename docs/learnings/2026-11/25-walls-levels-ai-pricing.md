# Walls levels: the AI must price cities net of its own Siege (2026-11-25)

Rules 58 lets a city's Walls reach level IX. The Hard AI's target and war arithmetic (`analysis::city_power`, `campaign` need and defenders, `conquest` cost) read the city's full strength (`CityView::strength`, `city_defense_for_rules` with the full fort tier). With nine levels that made every late city look unbreakable: in a 2-player Hard duel (seed 14, Sparta vs Switzerland) nobody declared war and the game ran to the 1,200-turn cap. Pricing rival cities with `city_defense_vs(.., covered = viewer_siege_cover)` (`analysis::city_strength_vs_viewer`) fixed it; 64 duels then had no stall. Any new city-defence layer must be priced against what the attacker's Siege covers, not at its full value. The 8-player census did not show it (no stalls either way); `crates/sim/tests/hold_metrics.rs` did.

`city_strength_vs_viewer` subtracts the covered levels' strength from the shown strength rather than taking the smaller of the shown and recomputed values: with real views the two are equal, but test views that set `strength` directly (e.g. `capitals::tests`, `conquest::tests`) need the shown strength kept.
